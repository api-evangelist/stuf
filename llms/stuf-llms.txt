# Stuf Storage

> Stuf is a next-generation self-storage company offering clean, secure, tech-enabled storage units in the neighborhoods where people live and work. Stuf partners with real estate owners to convert underutilized space in commercial buildings into modern storage facilities across 7 US metro areas: New York City, Los Angeles, the San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle, Atlanta, Boston, and Washington, DC. Members book online in minutes, pay month-to-month with no hidden fees, and access their unit with a digital key.

Stuf serves storage seekers (residents, small businesses, and students) looking for convenient self-storage near home or work, and commercial property owners looking to monetize underutilized space such as basements, garages, and retail vacancies.

**How booking works.** Stuf storage is booked entirely online: search available units by location, reserve, and move in with a digital key. Reserving a unit requires a $20 hold, which is applied to the first invoice and is fully refundable up to 48 hours before the selected move-in date. Move-in is available as soon as the next day. The first payment is due on move-in day, and subsequent payments are due on the first of each month. Leases are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Quoted rates include a complimentary lock, taxes, and cleaning and storage fees — no hidden or additional fees. Storage insurance is required: purchase coverage through Stuf or provide proof of your own insurance before move-in.

**How does move-in and access work?** After booking, the Stuf team confirms the reservation within 24–48 hours. Members unlock facility doors with a digital key from their Member Dashboard on a mobile device; every unit comes pre-locked with a complimentary combination lock whose code is stored in the dashboard. Access hours vary by location and are listed on each location's page.

**How do cancellation and move-out work?** Reservations can be cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours before the move-in date. Cancelling within 48 hours of move-in forfeits the $20 hold; cancelling on or after the move-in date incurs the first month's invoice. After move-in, rentals are month-to-month; move-out requires at least 24 hours' notice via the Member Dashboard, and the final month's rent is not prorated. Rate adjustments occur at most once every six months, with at least 30 days' notice.

**Does Stuf offer moving services?** Stuf does not operate its own moving service, but partners with moving companies in many cities to provide a seamless pickup, transport, and storage experience. Carts, dollies, and hand trucks are available on-site, first-come first-serve.

**What cannot be stored in a Stuf unit?** Vehicles (including e-bikes and scooters), food or animal feed, alcohol or drugs, firearms, ammunition, explosives, flammable or toxic materials, fuel, tires, batteries, propane, and irreplaceable items of sentimental value. Units may not be used for living or working.

**Unit sizes and what fits.** Stuf offers storage units from closet-sized to one-car-garage-sized. Most units are 8–10 feet tall.

- 3 x 5 (15 sq ft) — like a small walk-in closet: mattress, small dresser, boxes and bins
- 5 x 5 (25 sq ft) — like a standard closet: twin/full mattress, small dresser, seasonal items
- 5 x 10 (50 sq ft) — walk-in closet size: queen bed, sofa, dresser, bikes, moving boxes
- 6 x 10 (60 sq ft) — small bedroom size: queen bed, loveseat, desk, boxes
- 10 x 10 (100 sq ft) — standard bedroom size: contents of a one-bedroom apartment or small office
- 10 x 15 (150 sq ft) — large bedroom / small garage size: full bedroom and living room sets, appliances
- 10 x 20 (200 sq ft) — one-car garage size: full-home storage or business inventory, 40–50 boxes
